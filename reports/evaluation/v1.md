## How to check this

Every figure above is derived from the artifacts in `data/eval/v1/`: `scenarios.jsonl` holds the exact bytes each model was given, `generations.jsonl` the answers and telemetry, `checks.jsonl` the numeric verification, and `judgments.jsonl` the rubric verdicts with their justifications. The report recomputes from those files and adds nothing.
